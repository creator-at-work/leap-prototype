About this prototype

Leap is a chat-first, freemium study-abroad counselling app concept by Jatin Bhatt, with an AI counsellor called Leapo. This is a clickable prototype for a product assignment, not a live product.

AI disclosure

  • Built with the Grok Bot assistant.
  • Research was done with ChatGPT and Codex.
  • The in-app chat uses Google Gemini (gemini-3.8-flash) for this demo only, called server-side. Gemini's terms bar apps for under-18 users, so a production version would use Anthropic.
  • Leapo is told to use only the provided college data and never invent numbers. AI replies can still be wrong.

Sample data

  • College costs are all-in yearly figures (tuition plus living) from official 2026/27 pages: Purdue ₹54.5 lakh, UBC ₹49.0 lakh plus student fees and insurance, Georgia Tech ₹54.3 lakh. FX: USD 96.03, CAD 67.96 (mean of 14 Sep to 13 Oct 2026).
  • Names (Jatin, Maa), the counsellor on the success screen, and chat answers are example data.
  • The Razorpay checkout is a mock. No payment is taken.
  • Typed messages are sent to Google Gemini to generate replies. Please don't enter personal information.
